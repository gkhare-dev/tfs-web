9. Your Rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, you (the “Data Principal”) have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

Right to Access

Right to obtain a summary of the personal data we hold about you and the processing activities carried out on it.

Right to Correction and Erasure

Right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data, and to request erasure of personal data that is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected.

Right to Grievance Redressal

Right to have any complaint about our handling of your personal data addressed in a timely manner (see Section 10).

Right to Nominate

Right to nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Right to withdraw your consent to our processing of your personal data at any time, as easily as it was given. Withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal, and may affect our ability to continue providing certain services to you.
