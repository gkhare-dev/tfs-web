Who We Are

Meet the Founder — Prabhakar Khare

The Finance Suite was built around a simple belief: financial and lending decisions are better when they're guided by someone who has actually worked inside a bank.

Founded by Prabhakar Khare, a career banker with over 37 years of experience in Credit and Foreign Exchange, The Finance Suite brings insider banking knowledge directly to individuals and businesses in Noida and beyond.

Over more than three decades in banking, Prabhakar developed a deep, practical understanding of how credit decisions are really made — how banks assess risk, structure lending products, and manage foreign exchange transactions. That insider perspective is now at the heart of everything The Finance Suite does.

He holds a Master's degree (MA) in Economics and is CAIIB certified (Certified Associate of the Indian Institute of Bankers), reflecting a rigorous, professional standard carried through into every client engagement.
